# Community guidelines

We want DepPrism to be a useful, rigorous, and welcoming open-source project.

Be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, and make space for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these guidelines, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, private where practical, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to `opensource@nintech.io`. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, and reporter privacy will be respected to the extent possible.
